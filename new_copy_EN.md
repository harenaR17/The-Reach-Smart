### TAB: FOR SAAS BUSINESSES

#### AI Chat Support Agent

**Hook:** Stop solving the same problem a hundred times.

We build an AI agent trained on your documentation, knowledge base, previous conversations, and internal procedures.

The agent doesn't just send a link to the help center. It understands the question, asks clarifying questions, and walks the user step by step to a resolution.

When a case requires human judgment, the system transfers it to the right person along with the conversation context.

After your team resolves a new problem, the solution can be added to the knowledge base. Next time the same issue comes up, the agent already knows how to handle it.

**The result:** Your support team deals with new and complex cases instead of rewriting already-given answers.

#### Lead Finder System

**Hook:** Let your salespeople sell — not organize lists.

The system finds companies that match your ideal customer profile and identifies the people involved in the decision-making process.

Filtering can include industry, company size, location, technologies used, new hires, and other buying intent signals.

You get a structured, regularly updated list with:
- the right company;
- the relevant person;
- position and contact details;
- the reason the company was included.

**The result:** Your sales team starts the day with prospects, not a blank search field.

**Button:** See how it works

---

#### Lead Finder + Outreach System

**Hook:** From an empty pipeline to replies in your inbox.

We combine prospect finding with automated, personalized outreach.

The system researches the specific company and prepares a message based on their business, context, and likely need. It doesn't send the same template to every contact.

Follow-up messages are sent automatically. When someone replies, the automation stops and the conversation is handed to your team.

The system tracks:
- messages sent;
- deliverability;
- replies;
- positive interest;
- scheduled conversations.

You step in when there's a real reason for a conversation.

**The result:** Qualified opportunities come to the sales team, instead of the team constantly chasing them.

## HOW IT WORKS

## FAQ

**Headline:** Questions we answer directly

**Q: Which automation should we start with?**
The one that can return the most value with the least unnecessary complexity.

For one SaaS business, that might be customer support. For another, it's finding prospects. For an e-commerce brand, it might be creative production or post-purchase communication.

In the diagnostic call, we compare the options and rank them by potential impact.

**Q: How long does the build take?**
It depends on the number of integrations, the quality of available data, and the complexity of decisions the system needs to make.

Most standard automations can be launched within three weeks of scoping. More complex systems are built and launched in phases.

**Q: Will it work with the tools we already use?**
That's one of the first checks we do.

We build systems around your existing help desk, CRM, online store, knowledge base, and communication channels — when the necessary integrations are available.

If there's a technical limitation, we'll point it out before the project starts.

**Q: Do we need technical expertise on our side?**
No.

We handle design, build, integration, testing, and initial optimization.

From your team, we need knowledge of the business, processes, customers, and situations where a human needs to step in.

**Q: Will customers know they're talking to AI?**
Some probably will — and that's not necessarily a problem.

What matters more is that they get a fast, accurate, and helpful answer. In complex, sensitive, or unusual cases, the system must be able to transfer the conversation to a human.

We don't design the automation to deceive. We design it to help.

**Q: What happens if the system makes a mistake?**
We define upfront what it can do autonomously and what must be confirmed or handled by a human.

After launch, we monitor the system's real-world performance and fine-tune the problematic scenarios.

Human control and traceability are preserved for important actions.

**Q: What data will be needed?**
It depends on the automation.

For a support agent: documentation, FAQs, and previously resolved cases.
For lead generation: a description of the ideal customer profile.
For a UGC system: product information, customer questions, reviews, and previous ads.

We only use the data and access required for the specific process.

**Q: How much does it cost?**
The price depends on the complexity of the process, integrations, volume, and required maintenance.

After the diagnostic call, you receive a clear scope and price before making any decision.

We don't start building before you know exactly what you're getting.

**Q: How will we know if the automation is worth it?**
Before starting, we define the metrics that need to change.

This could be: response time, number of automatically resolved inquiries, hours of manual work, contacts found, positive replies, conversations scheduled, ad tests run, or repeat purchases.

After launch, we compare results against the baseline.
